THE CENTRAL REVELATION

Nothing Is Missing.
Something Is Misplaced.

In 2019, while seeking God for provision and direction during a project in Toronto, Apostle Bible Davids received an insight that reshaped his understanding of destiny:

“Everything you need already exists. It now has to come into alignment.”

The Law of Alignment

Life does not respond to effort alone. It responds to position. Alignment is the right person, in the right place, at the right time, doing the right things, with the right people, for the purpose heaven designed.

The issue is not always absence. Sometimes what you need exists—but you are not yet positioned to receive it.
