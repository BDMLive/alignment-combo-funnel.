THE CENTRAL REVELATION

Nothing Is Missing. Something Is Misplaced.

In 2019, while seeking God for provision and direction during a project in Toronto, Apostle Bible Davids received an insight that reshaped his understanding of destiny:

“Everything you need already exists. It now has to come into alignment.”

The Law of Alignment

Life does not respond to effort alone. It responds to position. Alignment is the right person, in the right place, at the right time, doing the right things, with the right people, for the purpose heaven designed.

The issue is not always absence. Sometimes what you need exists—but you are not yet positioned to receive it.

What is ALIGNMENT: What You Are Looking For Is Looking For You about?

ALIGNMENT explores the relationship between divine position and purpose. The author describes it as a diagnostic and compass for recognizing misalignment and returning to divine position, rather than a motivational book.

Who is the ALIGNMENT book for?

It is for readers seeking biblical understanding of purpose, identity, relationships and divine timing, and those reflecting on where they may need to return to alignment.

What will I discover inside the book?

The book addresses divine timing, purpose, identity, relationships, provision, spiritual authority, realignment, prayer and destiny recognition. It also includes personal diagnostics, prayers and declarations.
